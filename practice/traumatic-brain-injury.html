Key facts

Why TBI differs

Traumatic brain and catastrophic injuries often involve specialized medicine, disputed causation, and damages that unfold over time — not a quick soft-tissue pattern.

Damages

Where the facts and law support it, claims may include economic and non-economic losses. No dollar promises on this page.

Who we represent

We represent injured people and families pursuing recovery. This site is for plaintiffs — not insurers or defendants.

Why timing & records matter

Early medical records, investigation, and coverage review can affect options. Prompt legal review helps you understand the path — this page is not legal advice.
