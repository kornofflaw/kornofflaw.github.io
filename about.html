About Kornoff Law

California wrongful death and catastrophic injury litigation — personal attention on carefully selected matters.

Discuss your matterCall (402) 543-4452

Kornoff Law is the private practice of Andrew F. Kornoff, focused on California wrongful death and serious personal injury matters — including traumatic brain injury and other catastrophic injuries.

Andrew is admitted to practice in California state courts and in federal court. The practice is not a volume personal-injury pipeline: matters are selected carefully, and clients receive personal attention through investigation, strategy, and communication about risk and next steps.

Handles wrongful death and serious injury matters venued in Orange County and Los Angeles County — and elsewhere in California as the case requires.

When the engagement calls for it, the firm also handles related insurance bad-faith and coverage disputes arising from those matters — always for injured people and families, not insurers.
